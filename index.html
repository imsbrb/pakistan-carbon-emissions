What this suggests to monitor next

Three limits of national annual data, and what would close them.

  • Annual national totals cannot see a city. Urban policy acts on neighbourhoods and corridors, so the useful unit is a gridded inventory — satellite NO₂ and CO columns, or a bottom-up inventory built from traffic counts and industrial registers.
  • Production, not consumption. These figures count emissions where fuel is burned. Consumption-based accounting reassigns them to where goods are used, and changes who is responsible for what.
  • One value per year is too coarse for intervention. Hourly or daily series are what let you attribute a change to a policy, a fuel-price shock or a weather inversion.
